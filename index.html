Who I am

San Diego, CA

I'm pursuing a combined degree at UC San Diego — a B.S. in Computer Engineering (Dec 2026) and an M.S. in Machine Learning and Data Science (Dec 2027). My work spans building production MCP gateways and agent-evaluation frameworks at Primare, architecting autonomous research pipelines running 44,000+ backtests at WeInvest, and researching multi-agent simulation at Q-Lab — published as a NeurIPS 2025 Spotlight.

Outside of the lab, I lead CSES Innovate, a 40+ member student research organization focused on lowering the barriers to entry into AI research. When I'm not building, you'll find me playing chess, running, or on the squash court.
